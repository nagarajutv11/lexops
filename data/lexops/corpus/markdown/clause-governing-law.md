# Playbook: Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

**NORTHWIND SYSTEMS INTERNAL LEGAL PLAYBOOK**
**SECTION 14: GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

---

### 14.1 Preferred Northwind Position (Model Clause Language)

> **Section 14.1. Governing Law.** This Agreement, and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims), shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods shall not apply.
> 
> **Section 14.2. Dispute Resolution.**
> (a) **Negotiation.** In the event of any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement (a "Dispute"), each party shall designate a senior executive with authority to settle the Dispute. Such executives shall negotiate in good faith for a period of thirty (30) days to resolve the Dispute.
> (b) **Arbitration.** If the Dispute is not resolved through negotiation within thirty (30) days of the initial written notice of the Dispute, the Dispute shall be finally settled by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) under its Commercial Arbitration Rules. The number of arbitrators shall be one (1). The place of arbitration shall be Wilmington, Delaware. The language of the arbitration shall be English. The arbitrator shall issue a reasoned award.
> (c) **Injunctive Relief.** Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Section 14, either party may seek interim injunctive relief or other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to protect its intellectual property rights or confidential information without first complying with the negotiation provisions of Section 14.2(a).
> (d) **Class Action Waiver.** The parties agree that any arbitration or court proceeding shall be conducted on an individual basis only and not as a class, consolidated, or representative action.

---

### 14.2 Fallback Position

If the counterparty rejects Delaware law or binding arbitration, the legal team is authorized to apply the following cascading fallbacks without escalation:

1. **Governing Law:** Accept the laws of the State of New York, the State of California, or the State of Texas (for Texas-headquartered counterparties only).
2. **Venue / Forum:** If arbitration is unacceptable to the counterparty, accept state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware, or New York County, New York, as the exclusive forum.
3. **Dispute Resolution Notice Periods:** The negotiation cure period under Section 14.2(a) may be extended from thirty (30) days to forty-five (45) days upon counterparty request.

---

### 14.3 Walk-Away Position

The legal team must cease negotiations and walk away from the transaction, or escalate to the Chief Executive Officer, if the counterparty insists on any of the following terms:

1. **Foreign Governing Law:** Governing law of any jurisdiction outside the United States (except England and Wales, which is subject to escalation under Section 14.5).
2. **Uncapped Liability for Dispute Costs:** Any clause requiring Northwind to pay all attorneys' fees and arbitration costs of the counterparty regardless of the outcome of the dispute.
3. **Multi-Tiered Mandatory Litigation Prerequisities:** Any mandatory dispute resolution mechanism requiring more than sixty (60) days of pre-arbitration mediation or board-level escalation before a party can initiate formal legal proceedings.

---

### 14.4 Numeric and Categorical Thresholds

The following thresholds govern dispute resolution parameters across all Northwind enterprise customer and vendor agreements:

* **Monetary Floor for Arbitration:** Disputes involving a total claimed amount of less than **$50,000 USD** shall be resolved through binding expedited desk arbitration under the AAA procedures, without live hearings, unless both parties agree otherwise.
* **Notice Period for Dispute Initiation:** A party must provide written notice of a Dispute within **90 calendar days** of discovering the underlying act, omission, or default giving rise to the claim; failure to provide such notice constitutes a complete waiver of the claim.
* **Cure Period for Material Breaches:** Except for non-payment of fees (which carries a strict **10-day** cure period), all material breaches are subject to a mandatory **30-day** cure period following receipt of written notice before a Dispute may be formally declared under Section 14.2.
* **Arbitrage and Legal Fee Cost-Shifting Cap:** In any arbitration or legal proceeding arising out of this Agreement, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs, provided that such recovery shall be strictly capped at **15%** of the total annual contract value (ACV) in dispute.

---

### 14.5 Named Deviations and Escalation Matrix

Any contract variation falling outside the Fallback Position (Section 14.2) requires formal written approval from the designated internal authority before signature:

1. **Governing Law Deviation (Foreign Jurisdictions):** Acceptance of the laws of England and Wales, or any Canadian province (Ontario or British Columbia). 
   * *Escalation To:* **General Counsel (Marcus Vance)**.
2. **Venue Deviation (Local Counterparty Courts):** Acceptance of local state courts in the counterparty’s home state within the United States (other than Delaware, New York, California, or Texas).
   * *Escalation To:* **Deputy General Counsel (Sarah Jenkins)**.
3. **Alternative Dispute Resolution Provider:** Acceptance of Judicial Arbitration and Mediation Services (JAMS) or the International Institute for Conflict Prevention and Resolution (CPR) in lieu of the American Arbitration Association (AAA).
   * *Escalation To:* **Senior Commercial Counsel (David Ross)**.

### 14.6 Worked Examples of Counterparty Language

#### Acceptable Counterparty Language (Approved for inclusion without escalation)

* **Example A (Governing Law & Forum Fallback):**
  > *"This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York. The parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement."*
  * **Analysis:** This falls squarely within the authorized Fallback Position (Section 14.2). New York law and venue are acceptable alternatives to Delaware.

* **Example B (Extended Negotiation Period):**
  > *"Prior to initiating any binding arbitration, the parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement by prompt negotiation between executives who have authority to settle the controversy, which negotiation shall last for a period of up to forty-five (45) days."*
  * **Analysis:** This is acceptable under the fallback threshold. While the preferred period is thirty (30) days, Section 14.2 permits up to forty-five (45) days without escalation.

#### Unacceptable Counterparty Language (Must be rejected or escalated)

* **Example C (Unacceptable Foreign Law & Venue):**
  > *"This Agreement is governed by the laws of Switzerland, and any disputes shall be submitted exclusively to the courts of Zürich, Switzerland."*
  * **Analysis:** This violates the Walk-Away Position (Section 14.3) prohibiting foreign governing law outside the US (and unapproved UK/Canada). Must be rejected immediately.

* **Example D (Unilateral One-Sided Forum Selection):**
  > *"Northwind agrees that any legal action brought by Customer against Northwind may be instituted in any court of competent jurisdiction in Customer's home state, whereas any action brought by Northwind against Customer must be brought exclusively in the courts of Delaware."*
  * **Analysis:** This creates an asymmetrical, unbalanced dispute resolution mechanism that exposes Northwind to multi-jurisdictional litigation. This requires mandatory escalation to the General Counsel and will be rejected in standard negotiations.
